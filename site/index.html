The discipline layer.

This is the part that actually helps. Alerts are easy — anyone can fire a ping when a chart moves. The harder problem is what happens after: the urge to take a trade you already decided not to take, five minutes after telling yourself you wouldn't.

LOSS LIMITS & TRADE CAPS

You set a daily loss limit and a trade cap before the market opens — back when you're calm, not forty minutes into a losing streak. Once the session starts, neither one can go up. Ask to loosen either mid-day and the request queues for tomorrow morning, not right now.

TILT DETECTION

A few losses in a row, or a position sized bigger than the one that just hurt you — Kestrel notices, and says so, in the moment that usually gets talked over.

THE JOURNAL

Every trade you actually take gets logged automatically, the triggering alert attached. Once a month, it tells you where the money really went — including the specific, uncomfortable number: how you do on trades you take after it already told you not to.
