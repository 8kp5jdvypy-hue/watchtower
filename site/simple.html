How it works

Watch. Detect. Alert.

01

Watch

Perch reads live price action across the whole session, every few minutes, no breaks and no days off.

02

Detect

It measures each move against what's normal for that specific name, so you hear about the signal — not the noise.

03

Alert

You get a plain-English alert the moment it happens — on the web now, in the app soon. What you do next is up to you.
