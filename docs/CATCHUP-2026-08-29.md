## 3. The postmarket signal-quality / customer dry-run program

### What it is

A program (`docs/signal-quality-program.md`, 2026-08-28, plus
`docs/market-intelligence-program-2026-08.md`, 2026-08-26) to turn Perch from a
regular-session reaction detector into a market-wide observer whose coverage,
timeliness, tradability, ranking and forward outcomes are independently
measurable — with a hard rule that **every new capability stays shadow-only
until an evidence gate passes**, and that no score may be called confidence,
probability, profitability or advice unless its calibration supports that exact
claim.

Architecturally it is four default-off services in `docker-compose.yml`
alongside the existing `worker`/`bot`/`runner`/`api`/`caddy`:

| Service | Switch (default) | Command |
|---|---|---|
| `postmarket` | `POSTMARKET_SHADOW_ENABLED=0` | scheduled-earnings observer |
| `postmarket-discovery` | `POSTMARKET_DISCOVERY_ENABLED=0` | market-wide unscheduled discovery |
| `postmarket-external-context` | `POSTMARKET_EXTERNAL_CONTEXT_ENABLED=0` | slow options/news/SEC enrichment |
| `postmarket-customer-dry-run` | `POSTMARKET_CUSTOMER_DRY_RUN_ENABLED=0` | readiness router, ledger only |

### Why it was started

One causal chain, from real production evidence on 2026-08-26 (SOURCED,
`market-intelligence-program-2026-08.md`): CRWD, CRM and OKTA reported after the
close. Full postmarket bars were available for all three. There was no Alpaca or
SIP outage, no budget, cooldown, suppression or delivery problem — **no
detection existed to route.** The chain was: active universe → no market-wide
catalyst admission → not promoted into the 25-symbol Stage 2 set → no Stage 2
evaluation → no detection → no alert. And the regular-session runner terminates
at the XNYS close, so it could not have observed the reaction even if the
symbols had been promoted.

### Gates remaining before this touches a single subscriber

Nothing in this program can send. `postmarket_delivery_dry_run.py` produces only
`ELIGIBLE_FOR_DRY_RUN` or `SUPPRESSED` ledger rows. The owner field is named
`dry_run_readiness_approved` specifically so that approving it approves a
*readiness review*, not delivery.

**Technical gates still open:**

1. **≥10 clean live shadow sessions.** Currently **0**.
2. **≥95% recall** for scheduled liquid reactions reaching 8% on an
   independently labeled empirical holdout. Holdout is **empty**.
3. **Worst-case detection latency ≤330 s.** A real 182 s tick gap is logged as an
   open blocker (#96); the five-shard sweep (#129) has never run a complete
   prospective session.

## 7. Decisions a future planner must not reverse

Each with its one-line rationale. Sources are in-repo (code comments, doc
DECISIONS sections, PR bodies) unless noted.

**Data-population integrity**

1. **Never blend feed eras** (`CURRENT_FEED_FILTER_SQL`, "Decision B",
   2026-08-12). A feed change means different prices and volume baselines;
   blending fabricates a statistic from an incompatible population. Resetting
   the stats to near-zero was the accepted cost.
2. **Screening-origin (`broad_scan`-promoted) rows stay out of technical base
   rates** — extended to tier and hour statistics in #134. A different admission
   path is a different population, not more data.
3. **News-driven rows are excluded from the clean-technical population, and the
   excluded count is reported** (#134). Silently shrinking `n` hides the
   exclusion; reporting it keeps the omission visible.
4. **The SIP display path must not be folded into the detector feed**
   (PROGRAM-STATE.md guardrail). They answer different questions and have
   different calibration requirements; unifying them silently reintroduces the
   20–42× IEX/SIP volume-baseline mismatch that would make `rvol_spike` fire on
   almost everything.

**Honesty surfaces**

5. **The public track record is the ALERTED population** (owner decision
   2026-08-18, encoded in `api/app.py` and `rendering/recap.py`). The default —
   every HIGH-tier detection, alerted or not — would overstate what a subscriber
   actually received.
6. **Uptime is never fabricated as 100%** when no incident tracking has started
   (`StatusPageData.uptime_pct is None`). An absent measurement is not a perfect
   score.
7. **The 2026-08-26 postmarket session does not count as clean** despite being
   internally healthy — ~11% window coverage. Accepting it would have made the
   clean-session count a number that means nothing.
8. **Overlapping metric counters are explicitly disclosed as not a deduplicated
   incident total** (#137). Presenting a sum of overlapping families as an
   incident count would be a fabricated statistic.

**Detector and alerting behavior**

9. **Earnings are context AND signal, never suppression** (owner decision
   2026-08-17; `events.py`, `journal.py`). Earnings day and day-before carry
   `context` severity; only macro windows keep `suppress`.
10. **Spread guard = Option B, 15% cap, spread shown on the card** (owner
    decision 2026-08-17). Chosen over the alternative; RNWWW confirms it working
    as intended.
11. **A dedup-lookup crash defaults to WATCH, not suppress** — a deliberate
    exception to "always suppress on failure." A dedup bug says nothing about
    whether the underlying signal is trustworthy, unlike a stale quote or a
    missing bar; failing closed here would silently zero out all HIGH alerting
    on a transient bug.
12. **A guard-rejected HIGH refunds its budget reservation** (#84). A rejected
    alert never reached anyone, so it must not consume the daily cap that a real
    alert later needs.
13. **A batched MEDIUM is not a suppression** (#82). It took the designed route;
    calling it suppression would corrupt every miss-report bucket count.
14. **The bounded mover/active lane is retained alongside the five-shard sweep,
    not replaced** (#129). The bounded lane is the fast attention path; the sweep
    is the completeness path. They answer different questions.
15. **All thresholds stay in ATR units, detectors stay pure, every detection is
    journaled before any alert** (CLAUDE.md, load-bearing for
    `_commit_then_send()`).

**Program governance — the ones most likely to be "optimized" away**

16. **Everything postmarket stays shadow-only and default-off until its evidence
    gate passes.** *"No shadow milestone silently enables alerts."* Four
    independent kill switches, all defaulting to `0`, so a routine deploy cannot
    start anything by accident.
17. **`dry_run_readiness_approved` approves a readiness review, not delivery.**
    Deliberately named that way; activation must be a different, separately
    controlled owner action, and no dry-run result can create it.
18. **Campaigns are locked prospectively, before the first covered session**
    (#111, #114, #123). Locking the range, floors, providers and eligible
    revisions after outcomes are visible is how you pick a favorable window; the
    sealer refuses post-hoc artifact selection for the same reason.
19. **The rank is `heuristic_evidence_ordering_not_probability`** and may never
    be presented as confidence, probability, expected return, profitability or
    advice unless calibration supports that exact claim (#103, #130).
20. **Synthetic cases never count toward the activation gate** (#88). The
    empirical holdout stays empty until independently labeled *live* cases exist.
21. **Tuning examples are never independent evidence**; CRWD/CRM/OKTA are
    isolated as tuning fixtures.
22. **Licensed reference data must be contracted, never inferred** (#108).
    Sector must not be derived from SIC, and float must not be derived from SEC
    public-float value. Explicitly unavailable beats plausibly wrong.
23. **The second provider must be genuinely independent** (#106) — never a
    same-provider feed comparison dressed up as corroboration.
24. **The archive deletes nothing; pruning is separately gated** (#141, #142) on
    real production archive *and* restore reconciliation. Retention policy is not
    a licence to delete before restore is proven.
25. **`universe.db` is a required backup** (#141) — it now holds irrebuildable
    Stage-1 screening evidence, not just a rebuildable asset catalog.

**Operations**

26. **All deploys go through `scripts/deploy.sh` with a full 40-character SHA**
    (#140). Reconstructing the steps manually is what produced
    `GIT_SHA=unknown` in every production detection row before 2026-08-12, which
    then hampered that incident's own postmortem. systemd boot must never
    rebuild images.
27. **Rollback is ancestor-only, to a reviewed revision.** No arbitrary SHA.
28. **`scripts/` tools run in-container**, never against bare host `python3` —
    the host has no deps and the image has no `scripts/`. The one canonical
    invocation is in DEPLOYMENT.md; the VPS hit both failure modes on 2026-08-17.

30. **There is no cache-purge step for the Workers frontends.** Zone Cache Rules
